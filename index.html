A Guide to Demon Slaying

「 KNYDEX 」

The Demon Slayer Corps consists of trained swordsmen who use breathing styles to effectively fight demons. The Hashiras, or Pillars, are the highest amongst their ranks, and directly answer the Master, Kagaya Ubuyashiki.

The demons are beings under the demon king's (Muzan Kibutsuji) control with extraordinary strength and abilities. They burn under sunlight, which is why, for centuries, Muzan has sought out the blue spider lily to gain immunity to the sun. The Twelve Kizuki, or Demon Moons, are Muzan's twelve strongest demons, divided into sub-groups, the Lower and Upper Moons. The Upper Moons are Muzan's six strongest demons, who have received the greatest amount of his blood.

The Demon Slayer Corps have been fighting demons for almost 1,000 years in hopes of finally defeating Muzan. To end this relentless war, we need your help. KNYDEX is a guide to teach and inform you about the slayers, demons, and breathing techniques before you start your training. Using KNYDEX, we will also decide the breathing technique that is best suited for you. Remember that this journey will be a rough one, but you are far more capable than you think.

“Set your heart ablaze.”
